Questions? Answers.

How is this different from Duolingo or Anki?
Duolingo requires 10-20 minute sessions. Anki lives on desktop. Wisentry works in 3-second glances on your lock screen — no app opening required. It fits into the moments between things you're already doing.
Do notifications work when my phone is locked?
Yes. You can see the flashcard and rate it directly from your lock screen without unlocking your phone. That's the whole point — zero friction.
Can I import my Anki decks?
Yes. You can import word lists from Anki, Quizlet, or any CSV file. Your existing vocabulary work isn't wasted.
What languages are supported?
Currently: English, Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish, and Polish. More languages are on the way.
Is my data private?
Your word lists stay on your device. We don't sell data, period. You can export everything at any time.
